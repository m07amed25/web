# **Mount Sinai & St. Catherine Monastery Tour - Witness the Sacred Sunrise**  

## **Overview**  
🌄 **Experience the breathtaking sunrise** from the summit of **Mount Sinai**, the revered site where **Moses received the 10 Commandments**.  

🚐 **Tour Highlights:**  
- Travel in **air-conditioned comfort** with a **personal Egyptologist guide**.  
- **Climb Mount Sinai** under the stars and witness **one of the most stunning sunrises** in the world.  
- Explore **St. Catherine’s Monastery**, a UNESCO World Heritage site and **home to the Burning Bush**.  
- Visit the **Greek Orthodox chapel**, famous for its **ancient icons and the Mosaic of Transfiguration**.  

---

## **Itinerary**  
⏰ **Pickup Time:** **9:00 PM** – Depart from your hotel in Sharm El Sheikh.  
🚐 **Drive to Catherine City (~3 Hours)** – Time may vary due to convoys.  
🧗 **Climb Mount Sinai (~3 Hours)** – A **7 km trek plus 500 steps** to reach the summit.  
🌅 **Enjoy the Sunrise** – Rest at the summit and admire the **breathtaking views**.  
⛰️ **Descend the Mountain** – Head back down to **St. Catherine’s Monastery**.  
🏛️ **Monastery Tour** – Explore its **ancient relics and historical treasures**.  
🚐 **Return Journey (~3 Hours)** – Travel back to **Sharm El Sheikh**.
