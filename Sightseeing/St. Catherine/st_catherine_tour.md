# **St. Catherine Monastery Tour - Sinai's Hidden Treasure**  

## **Overview**  
🏛️ **Explore the historic St. Catherine Monastery**, one of the world's **oldest Christian monasteries** and a sacred site for **Christianity, Islam, and Judaism**. Built in the **4th century**, this **UNESCO World Heritage Site** is home to an order of monks who have lived there for centuries.  

🚐 **Pickup & Transfer:**  
- **Pickup from your hotel** at **6:30 AM**.  
- **Air-conditioned van ride (~2.5 hours)** through the **beautiful Sinai desert**.  
- **Stop at interesting spots**, including a **small town for souvenirs**.  

🕌 **Tour Highlights:**  
1️⃣ **St. Catherine Monastery** – The **oldest working Christian monastery** in the world.  
2️⃣ **The Burning Bush** – Said to be the **Biblical bush where God spoke to Moses**.  
3️⃣ **The Skull Room** – A **room containing the bones of all the monks** who lived there (not always open).  
4️⃣ **A Piece of St. Catherine’s Finger Bone** – A **relic on display inside the monastery**.  
5️⃣ **A Mosque Inside the Monastery** – A unique place of worship inside the complex.  
6️⃣ **Dahab City** – Visit the **biggest shopping area in South Sinai**, famous for **handicrafts, jewelry, perfumes, carpets, and papyrus**.  
7️⃣ **Lunch in Dahab** – Enjoy a delicious meal at a **local restaurant**.  

After exploring Dahab, you will be **driven back to your hotel in Sharm El Sheikh** (~90-minute ride).
