# **Jerusalem Tour - The Holy City**  

## **Overview**  
🏛️ **Discover the ancient city of Jerusalem!** One of the **oldest cities in the world**, Jerusalem is sacred to **Judaism, Christianity, and Islam**. Both **Israelis and Palestinians claim Jerusalem** as their capital, making it a city rich in **history, culture, and spirituality**.  

🚐 **Pickup & Transfer:**  
- **Pickup from your hotel** at **9:00 PM** on **Monday or Thursday**.  
- **Air-conditioned bus ride (~2.5 hours)** to **Taba City**.  
- **Cross the Egyptian & Israeli borders** with assistance from our guides.  
- **Meet your English-speaking guide in Israel** and start the tour.  

🏝️ **Tour Highlights:**  
1️⃣ **The Dead Sea** – Float effortlessly in the world's saltiest body of water.  
2️⃣ **Mount of Olives** – Enjoy a spectacular view over the **Old City & the Temple Mount**.  
3️⃣ **Gethsemane** – Visit the site where **Jesus spent his last night before arrest**.  
4️⃣ **Mount Zion** – Explore **The Last Supper Room** and **Dormition Abbey**.  
5️⃣ **Western Wall** – Witness the **holiest site in Judaism**.  
6️⃣ **Via Dolorosa** – Walk the **path Jesus took carrying the Cross**.  
7️⃣ **Church of the Holy Sepulcher** – Visit the site of **Jesus’ crucifixion and tomb**.  
8️⃣ **Jaffa Gate** – Exit through one of the most important historical gates of Jerusalem.  

After the tour, you will **return to the Israel Border**, then cross back into Egypt. Our local guide will be **waiting in Taba City to transfer you back to your hotel** in Sharm El Sheikh.
