# **Petra Tour - The Rose City**  

## **Overview**  
🏜️ **Explore the Ancient City of Petra!** Discover the breathtaking **rock-cut architecture, water conduit system, and the lost city of the Nabataeans** in southern Jordan.  

🚐 **Pickup & Transfer:**  
- You will be picked up from your **hotel in Sharm** and driven to **Nuweibaa harbour** to board the ferry to Aqaba.  
- Upon arrival at the **Royal Yacht Club**, our representatives will assist you with arrival formalities.  
- You will then be **transferred by a modern coach to Petra** for a full-day guided tour.  

🏛️ **Tour Highlights:**  
1️⃣ **The Lost City** – Step into a historical wonder carved into the rose-red cliffs.  
2️⃣ **The SIQ Crack** – Walk through the **1-kilometer-long canyon** leading to Petra.  
3️⃣ **El Khazneh (The Treasury)** – Witness the **140-ft-high, 90-ft-wide** majestic monument.  
4️⃣ **Ancient Nabataean Structures** – Explore **temples, tombs, and a Roman theater**.  

At the end of your journey, you will be **transferred back to Aqaba** for departure from the **Royal Yacht Club**, with full assistance during departure formalities.
