# Canyon & Blue Hole Adventure  

## Overview

Your journey begins with a comfortable **air-conditioned van pickup** from your hotel, followed by a **two-hour drive** to the breathtaking **Coloured Canyon**. Located **10 km south of Dahab**, this deep desert oasis offers a truly unique experience.  

### **Explore the Coloured Canyon**  
- Spend **45 minutes** walking through the stunning canyon.  
- Enjoy the **spectacular rock formations** and their extraordinary colors.  
- In winter, witness **water oozing from the cracks** in the mountains.  
- Complimentary **refreshments (water) provided**.  

### **Camel Ride Along the Aqaba Gulf**  
- After a **45-minute drive** to Dahab city, embark on a **20-minute camel ride** along the beautiful **shoreline of the Aqaba Gulf**.  
- Marvel at the **crystal-clear waters** of the **Red Sea** as you ride.  

### **Snorkeling at the Blue Hole**  
- Prepare for an **unforgettable 45-minute snorkeling experience** at the famous **Blue Hole**, one of the world’s best spots for diving and snorkeling.  

### **Lunch & Relaxation**  
- After snorkeling, unwind in a **Bedouin tent by the sea**.  
- Enjoy a **delicious lunch** with stunning seaside views (**drinks not included**).  

### **Shopping in Dahab**  
- End your adventure with a visit to **Dahab’s bazaar**, where you can explore **local markets** and shop for souvenirs.  
- After shopping, you’ll be driven back to your hotel.
