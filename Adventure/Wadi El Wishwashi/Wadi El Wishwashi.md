
# Wadi El Wishwashi
## **Overview**

**Discover the Enchanting Wadi El Weshwash: A Nature Lover’s Dream**  

Embark on an unforgettable adventure to **Wadi El Weshwash**, a hidden gem of breathtaking landscapes, adventure, and tranquility. Perfect for nature lovers, thrill-seekers, and those in need of relaxation, this experience combines desert safari thrills, mesmerizing canyons, and the magical **Wishwashy Lake**, where you can swim in crystal-clear waters.  

### **Your Journey Begins**  
- **Early Morning Pickup**: We’ll pick you up from your hotel in Sharm El Sheikh in a comfortable, air-conditioned van and head to Dahab City.  
- **Scenic Stop in Nuweiba**: Take in panoramic views of Nuweiba, capturing stunning photographs before switching to a 4×4 jeep for the next part of the journey.  

### **Adventure in the Canyon & Wishwashy Lake**  
- **Hiking & Exploration**: Trek through the stunning canyon, a natural wonder carved by time.  
- **Swim in Wishwashy Lake** *(seasonal & optional)*: If the lake is full, take a refreshing swim in its tranquil waters.  
- **Unforgettable Views**: Enjoy breathtaking scenery and capture picture-perfect moments.  

### **Relaxation at Ras Shitan**  
- **Tea by the Sea**: Take a short break at Ras Shitan’s camp, sipping traditional Bedouin tea with a stunning sea view.  

### **Snorkeling & Lunch at Three Pools**  
- **Explore Dahab’s Famous Three Pools**: A paradise for snorkelers, this site offers crystal-clear waters teeming with marine life.  
- **Traditional Bedouin Lunch** *(drinks not included)*: Recharge with a delicious meal in an authentic Bedouin tent by the sea.  

### **Dahab City & Shopping Experience**  
- **Stroll Along the Promenade**: Discover the charming coastal city of Dahab.  
- **Bazaars & Local Markets**: Shop for unique souvenirs, handmade crafts, and traditional goods.  

### **Return to Sharm El Sheikh**  
- After a day filled with adventure and relaxation, we’ll drive you back to your hotel, arriving around **6:00 PM**.  

Don’t miss this incredible journey through Egypt’s hidden natural treasures—**a perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and culture!**
