# Ras Mohamed Boat Trip  

## Overview  

A fantastic boat ride to the beautiful **National Park of Ras Mohamed** to snorkel and enjoy the stunning **sea life and corals of the Red Sea**, with three different stops for snorkeling.  

You will be collected from your hotel in the morning around **8:00 AM** and drive to the **sea dock**, where you will board the boat and sail towards **Ras Mohamed National Park** for **three different snorkeling stops**.  

### **Snorkeling Stops**  
- **First Stop** – 45 minutes at one of the best snorkeling spots in the park to see colorful fishes, corals, and reefs.  
- **Second Stop** – 30 minutes sail to another beautiful coral reef full of marine life.  
- **Lunch Break** – Enjoy a **buffet lunch** on board before the third stop.  
- **Third Stop – White Island** – Spend about **1 hour** at the stunning White Island for relaxation and fun.  

### **Optional SCUBA Diving**  
- **Price:** £15 per dive for **10 minutes** (includes full diving equipment).  
- **Advance Booking Recommended** – If booked on the boat, the price may be higher.  

### **Tour Ends**  
- Arrive back at **4:30 PM** and transfer to your hotel.
