# **Luxury Sailing Boat Sina Dream - Sharm El Sheikh**  

## **Overview**  
Experience a **luxurious sailing adventure** on the **Sina Dream**, exploring the best **snorkeling sites** in the **Red Sea**, including **Ras Mohamed National Park** and **White Island**.

### **Itinerary**  
🚐 **Hotel Pickup** – Pickup from your hotel at **8:00 AM**, followed by a drive to the **sea dock**.  
⛵ **Sailing to Ras Mohamed** – Enjoy breathtaking **views of the coastline**.  
🌊 **First Snorkeling Stop (1.5 Hours)** – Explore the **vibrant coral reefs** and marine life at **Ras Mohamed**.  
🏝 **Second Stop at White Island** – Travel by **speedboat** or **zodiac** to **White Island**, where you can **relax on the beach**.  
🍽 **Lunch on Board** – Indulge in a **freshly cooked buffet** with a variety of **seafood and traditional dishes**.  
🛥 **Leisure & Relaxation** – Enjoy the **comfortable upper deck**, sun loungers, and **swings over the sea**.  
🚐 **Return to Hotel** – Arrive back at your hotel in **Sharm El Sheikh**.
