# **Semi Submarine Tour - Sharm El Sheikh**  

## **Overview**  
🌊 **Explore the Red Sea like never before!** Stay **completely dry** while enjoying the **vibrant marine life, colorful corals, and exotic fish** from a **comfortable air-conditioned armchair** inside a **semi-submarine**.  

This excursion is perfect for those who **want to experience the underwater world without getting wet**!  

🚐 **Pickup:** You will be collected from your hotel and driven to the sea dock, where you will board the semi-submarine.  

🛥️ **Tour Details:**  
- The **first part** of the journey takes you **3 meters underwater** for **60 minutes**, where you can view the stunning sea life and corals through **18 panoramic windows**.  
- The **second part** is a **30-minute** cruise on the upper deck, offering breathtaking views of **Sharm El Maya Bay** and the surrounding **mountains**.  

📸 Capture **amazing underwater photos** as you witness the **beauty of the Red Sea** in comfort.  
At the end of the tour, you will be **transferred back to your hotel**.
