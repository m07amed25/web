# **Intro Diving & Snorkeling Adventure - Sharm El Sheikh**  

## **Overview**  
Discover the breathtaking underwater world of the **Red Sea**, one of the **best diving locations in the world**! Whether you're a **first-time diver** or just looking to refresh your skills, this trip offers an unforgettable experience.  

🛥 **Full-Day Boat Trip** - Enjoy a full day on the sea with **multiple diving & snorkeling stops**.  
🤿 **Introductory Dive** - Perfect for **novices and beginners**, guided by a **professional dive master**.  
🐠 **Snorkeling Stops** - Explore vibrant **coral reefs & marine life** in two additional snorkeling locations.  
🍽 **Delicious Lunch Onboard** - Enjoy a **fresh buffet meal** and unlimited drinks.

### **Itinerary**  
🚐 **Hotel Pickup** – Pickup from your hotel at **8:00 AM**, followed by a drive to the **sea dock**.  
⛵ **Sailing to the Coral Reef Area** – Board the boat and head to the first stop.  
🤿 **First Stop: Intro Dive** – A professional **dive master** will guide you one-on-one for your first **10-minute dive**.  
🐠 **Second & Third Stops: Snorkeling** – Enjoy **two snorkeling locations**, filled with **colorful coral reefs & marine life**.  
🍽 **Lunch Onboard** – Relish a **buffet lunch** with soft drinks, tea, and coffee.  
🚐 **Return to Hotel** – Arrive back at your hotel by **5:00 PM**.
