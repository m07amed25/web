# VIP Boat Trip to Tiran Island  

## Overview  

Embark on a **luxurious boat trip** to **Tiran Island**, one of the **top snorkeling destinations** in the Red Sea. Explore the vibrant **coral reefs**, encounter stunning **marine life**, and enjoy a **relaxing day at sea** with three different snorkeling stops.  

### **About Tiran Island**  
Tiran Island is situated at the **southern tip of the Gulf of Aqaba**, where the waters are **2000 meters deep**. As the Gulf narrows, the sea becomes shallower, creating a **diverse underwater ecosystem** perfect for snorkeling and diving.  

### **Itinerary**  
🚐 **Hotel Pickup** – Pick up from your hotel around **8:00 AM** and a **20-minute drive** to the sea dock.  
🛥 **Sailing to Tiran Island** – Enjoy a **relaxing boat ride** with refreshments available on board.  
🌊 **Three Snorkeling Stops** – Discover the **vibrant coral reefs** and diverse **aquatic life** at different locations.  
🍽 **Buffet Lunch on Board** – Enjoy a **delicious meal** with **Chicken, Beef, Rice, Pasta, and Vegetable Salad**.  
🤿 **Optional SCUBA Diving** – Dive for **10 minutes** for an extra **£15 per person**, including all equipment. *(Advance booking recommended to avoid higher prices on the boat.)*  
🏖 **Relax and Enjoy the Sea** – Sunbathe, swim, or simply take in the breathtaking views.  
🚐 **Return to Hotel** – Arrive back at your hotel around **5:00 PM**.
